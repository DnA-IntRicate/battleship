\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\begin{guidance}[Conclusion (5 marks)]
Summarise \textbf{success} (which requirements you met, with evidence),
\textbf{usefulness} (where this design would apply beyond the project),
\textbf{limitations} (be honest: what does not work, or only works under
conditions) and \textbf{possible improvements}. No new results here.
\end{guidance}

\ph{What you achieved, tied back to the requirements and to your evidence.}

\textbf{Usefulness.} \ph{Where the same design ideas would apply.}

\textbf{Limitations.} \ph{What your system does not handle, and why.}

\textbf{Improvements.} \ph{What you would do next, e.g. a stretch feature
from the brief (AI opponent, two-client play, move clock, undo, hidden
information).}
